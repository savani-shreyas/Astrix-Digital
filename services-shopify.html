TECHNICAL SCOPE

What our Shopify development covers.

Every storefront is handcrafted for high conversions, rapid page loads, and effortless content updates by your merchandising team.

Custom Theme Architecture

We write clean, modular Shopify 2.0 themes from scratch using Liquid and JSON templates. You get complete visual differentiation without marketplace theme bloat.

Modular Sections & Blocks

Full control in the Shopify Theme Editor. Reorder sections, swap banners, adjust color schemes, and publish seasonal promotions without writing a line of code.

Custom Product & Collection Templates

Bespoke variant selectors, dynamic size guides, interactive specification tabs, shoppable lookbooks, and customized bundle flows.

Instant AJAX Slideout Cart

Smooth side-drawer cart with tier-based free shipping progress bars, dynamic upsell recommendations, and instant quantity recalculations.

Third-Party App Integrations

Seamlessly wire Klaviyo, customer reviews (Yotpo/Judge.me), subscriptions (Recharge), analytics, and customer support widgets without dragging down your speed.

Speed & Core Web Vitals QA

Critical CSS inline rendering, next-gen image formats (AVIF/WebP), deferred scripts, and responsive picture tags to ensure fast mobile checkout.
