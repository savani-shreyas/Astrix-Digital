Discover

  • Requirements specification
  • Architecture & tech stack audit
  • User journey mapping
  • Project timeline & milestones
02 Phase 2

Design

Key Deliverables:
  • High-fidelity Figma mockups

Develop

Launch & Refine

Test the experience, resolve issues, prepare the launch, and identify future improvements.

Key Deliverables:
  • Final QA & accessibility audit
  • Domain DNS & production deployment
  • Client video documentation walkthrough
  • Post-launch monitoring & support
